## Item by item

**[P3] §15 summary of invalid-projection rows: accepted, with the suggested wording.**

Rev 4's §15 row said these rows are "never extrapolated" and "have no position". That was broader than §7.7 and §9, as the review says:
- an interior `box_rejected` row with `kinematic` support can receive an admitted M7 position;
- an unmeasured interior row receives the smoother's prediction.

Only `seg = −1` rows are guaranteed to keep NaN positions.

Rev 4.1 uses the review's wording for the **Change** column: no box-derived measurements; prefixes, suffixes and unbridged runs get `seg = −1` and no position; interior rows may be predicted or receive admitted M7 measurements under §7.7.

I also corrected the **Effect** column. Rev 4 said the study "smoothed display segments across such rows". More precisely, the study built segments from the boxes, not from the projections, so invalid-projection rows at a display segment's **ends** were predicted too. Here only interior rows are. That is the actual difference from the study.
